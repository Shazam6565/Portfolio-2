An autonomous urban-intelligence prototype that turns natural-language questions into live geospatial analysis.

[Google ADK](https://google.github.io/adk-docs/) agents and the [Gemini Live API](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/live) reason over city data and drive a [CesiumJS](https://cesium.com/platform/cesiumjs/) 3D globe in real time, backed by a Python FastAPI service and a Next.js / TypeScript front end. The focus is agent orchestration and streaming, multimodal interaction over an interactive map. It is a step toward the closed-loop, real-world systems behind my Physical AI work.
