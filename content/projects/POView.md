Geospatial intelligence platform that merges a real-time 3D globe with a voice-driven AI assistant powered by Google's Gemini Live API. Users explore neighborhoods through cinematic drone flyovers — high orbit, sweeping approach, street-level arrival — while conversing naturally with an agent that pulls live weather, Places data, and AI-generated neighborhood profiles. Built on CesiumJS with Google 3D Photorealistic Tiles and a Python FastAPI backend orchestrated via Google ADK.
